Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 October 2026

This policy covers the mobile game Base Jump on Android and iOS. It explains what the game stores on your device, what our advertising provider collects, and what you can decide for yourself.

What the game collects

Nothing. Base Jump has no account, no sign-in and no server of its own. The game does not send your data anywhere.

Your best scores and your settings are written to a file on your own device. They stay on that device, they are not uploaded, and they are removed when you uninstall the game. We cannot read them.

What advertising collects

Base Jump is free and shows ads. Ads are served by Google through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. To do that, Google may collect:

  • advertising and device identifiers
  • device and operating system information
  • your IP address, which gives an approximate location
  • whether an ad was shown, viewed or tapped

This is used to deliver ads, to measure them and to prevent fraud. Where you have given permission, it is also used to personalise them. We do not receive this data ourselves and we do not sell data to anyone.

Google explains its own handling here: Google Privacy & Terms and how Google uses data in AdMob.

Your choices

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the game asks for your consent before any personalised advertising, using Google's consent form. You can change your answer at any time from the game's settings.

On iOS the system asks whether the game may track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you say no, you still see ads, they are simply not personalised. You can change this in iOS Settings under Privacy & Security, Tracking.

On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in the system settings.

Declining personalised ads does not limit the game in any way.

Purchases

Base Jump offers a one-time purchase that removes interstitial ads. The purchase is handled entirely by Google Play or the App Store. We never see or store your card details, and the store tells us only whether the purchase is active.

Children

Base Jump is intended for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has given us data, write to us and we will look into it.

How long data is kept

We keep nothing, because we receive nothing. The data on your device lasts until you uninstall the game. Data collected for advertising is kept by Google under its own retention rules.

Your rights

Under the GDPR and, in Türkiye, under the KVKK, you may ask what data is held about you, ask for it to be deleted or corrected, and object to its use. Since we hold no data ourselves, requests about advertising data need to go to Google. We will help you find the right place if you are not sure.

Changes

If this policy changes we update the date at the top of this page. Material changes will also be noted in the game's store listing.
