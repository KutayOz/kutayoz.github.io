Support

Last updated 26 October 2026

Contact

Write to [[SUPPORT-EMAIL]] and we will get back to you. English and Turkish are both fine.

My scores are gone

Scores and settings are stored only on your device. There is no account and no cloud backup, so uninstalling the game, resetting the device or moving to a new phone leaves them behind. We cannot restore them, because we never had a copy.

I bought the no-ads pack but I still see ads

Open the game's settings and use the restore option. Make sure the device is signed in to the same Google Play or App Store account you bought it with. The purchase is tied to that store account, not to the device.

If it still does not come back, send us the store receipt and we will look into it.

I want to change my ad settings

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the game's settings include the consent choice you made the first time you played. You can change it there.

On iOS, whether the game may track you across other companies' apps is an iOS setting, under Privacy & Security, Tracking. On Android you can reset your advertising ID in the system settings.

The game crashes or runs badly

Base Jump has no crash reporting, so nothing reaches us automatically. Your message is the only way we find out. Please include:

  • the device model
  • the Android or iOS version
  • what you were doing when it happened
  • roughly how high you were, if it happened during a run

Reporting a bug or suggesting something

Both are welcome at the same address. If something looks wrong on screen, a screenshot saves a lot of guessing.
